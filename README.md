# Willow & Leather: Cricket Club Manager 🏏

> *"Found a cricket club on the village green and take it all the way to Lord's!"*  
> Inspired by the acclaimed design and gameplay mechanics of [Flicco Manager](https://manager.flicco.io/game/).

---

## 🌟 Overview & Key Features

**Willow & Leather** is a retro-styled, tactile cricket management simulation game built to run seamlessly in any browser. It captures the charm of English village green cricket and scales up all the way to floodlit international stadiums.

### 1. 🎨 Authentic "Flicco Manager" Aesthetic
- **Warm Paper / Linen Palette**: `#fbf4e4` cream background, deep ink charcoal text, crisp brass/gold coins, and cricket ball cherry crimson (`#c73824`).
- **Tactile 3D Buttons & Cards**: Physical click feedback, bevelled pill badges, smooth spring transitions, and interactive segmented controls.
- **Dark Mode Support**: Seamless toggle between Vintage Cream Parchment and Night Match Floodlight mode (persisted in `localStorage`).
- **Web Audio API Sound Synthesizer**: 100% code-synthesized realistic sounds with zero external audio files needed (wooden willow bat cracks, bails flying/stumps rattling, crowd cheers, umpire whistles, and coin chimes).

### 2. ⚡ The "Big Chance" Interactive Match Engine
Just like Flicco's signature goal-scoring chances, matches simulate with ball-by-ball commentary and wagon wheel tracking, but **pause at critical high-leverage moments for YOU to play!**
- **Batting Big Chance**:
  - Face dangerous yorkers, bouncers, and outswingers.
  - Choose your shot: *Cover Drive*, *Pull/Hook*, *Lofted Straight Drive*, *Helicopter Flick*, *Late Cut*, or *Forward Defensive*.
  - Interactive **Oscillating Sweet-Spot Timing Meter**: Hit the green zone with your mouse or **Spacebar** to send the ball into the stands for SIX or crack it past extra cover for FOUR!
- **Bowling Big Chance**:
  - Deliver toe-crushing yorkers, sharp curving outswingers, or disguised slower balls.
  - Release timing meter tests your bowling accuracy to rattle the wooden timber or draw an outside edge!

### 3. 🏟️ Village Green to Lord's Infrastructure Journey
- **Ground Progression**:
  1. *The Village Green* (Cap: 220, Tea tent & white picket boundary)
  2. *Township Oval* (Cap: 950, Wooden stands & pavilion bar)
  3. *County Grounds* (Cap: 4,800, Covered grandstand & sight screens)
  4. *Metropolitan Arena* (Cap: 18,500, Towering floodlights & electronic screens)
  5. *Lord's Colosseum* (Cap: 46,000, World-class cathedral of cricket)
- **Club Facilities**: Upgrade Practice Nets (XP boost), Physiotherapy Suite (stamina recovery), Master Pitch Curator (customize pitch type), and Youth Scouting Academy.

### 4. 📋 Squad, Tactics & Transfer Market
- Complete starting squad of 15 players with unique roles (Opening Batter, Anchor, Finisher, Wicketkeeper, All-Rounder, Fast Seamer, Swing Bowler, Mystery Spinner).
- Core attributes: Batting Timing, Power, Bowling Speed, Accuracy, Fielding, Stamina, Form, and Traits (e.g. *Clutch Finisher*, *Death Yorker Specialist*, *Spin Magician*).
- Interactive lineup management, captaincy assignment, and tactical approach toggles.
- Live Scouting & Transfer Market to sign free agents and teenage village prodigies.

### 5. 🏆 4-Tier League Pyramid
- Division 4: Village Sunday League
- Division 3: District Championship
- Division 2: County Championship
- Division 1: Premier Super League
- Complete standings table with P, W, L, Tied, Net Run Rate, Points, and Orange/Purple cap leaderboards.

## 🚀 How to Run & Play

No installation, build steps, or external dependencies are required.

Simply open [`index.html`](index.html) in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox), or serve it with any local web server.

### Controls:
- **Mouse / Touch**: Tap to navigate tabs, assign tactics, upgrade grounds, and interact with the match.
- **Spacebar**: Tap to execute shots during Batting Big Chances when the meter enters the green sweet spot!
